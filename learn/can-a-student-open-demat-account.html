Can a student open a demat account?

By Ali Arbab, founder of StockSaathi · Updated 7 November 2026

Yes, if you are 18 or over. Being a student doesn't stop you: the rules look at your age and your KYC, and SEBI's booklet doesn't list a job among the requirements. Under 18, a demat account can exist in your name, but a guardian operates it and you can't trade through a broker.

Key takeaways

  • Under the Majority Act, a person in India reaches majority at 18.
  • A student aged 18 or over opens a demat account through the same KYC process as any adult.
  • Under 18, a demat account can be opened in your name but must be operated by a guardian.
  • A Basic Services Demat Account has no annual charge up to ₹4 lakh, and holdings can't exceed ₹10 lakh.

What can students do at each age?

What you can do depends on your age, not on whether you are in school or college.

Can a student open a demat account?

A student who is 18 or over can open one in the normal way, because the account depends on being an adult who can complete KYC. You need a bank account, a trading account with a SEBI-registered broker if you want to trade, and a demat account with a Depository Participant, as SEBI's investor booklet explains. Your status as a student doesn't enter into it. In practice, most students will need a PAN card and proof of address, and they should expect to complete the process online through e-KYC. You will need your own bank account too, and it is worth using one you control, because money from your investments moves through it.

Providers may ask for more than the minimum for certain products. Trading in derivatives is one area where SEBI's booklet says understanding of the products is required, and providers can apply further checks there, so ask before you assume anything. If a provider's process and this guide differ, follow what the provider and SEBI say today, since rules change.

What changes at age 18?

At 18 you can sign contracts, including one with a stock broker. Under the Majority Act, 1875, a person reaches majority at 18. If you already have a minor's demat account, SEBI's FAQ says it can continue after you turn 18, once you confirm the balance and complete the account-opening formalities.

How does a minor account work?

SEBI's FAQ says a demat account can be opened in a minor's name and operated by a guardian until the minor becomes a major. A minor can't be a joint holder. The guide for teenagers covers this in full, including who counts as a guardian.

What documents are needed?

KYC documents covering proof of identity and proof of address. SEBI's booklet gives examples such as a PAN card, Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or driving licence, and it says KYC is mandatory under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002. The demat account guide explains what the account does.

Does being a student change anything?

Not in the rules, but it does change what makes sense for you. Students often have small, irregular amounts and may need their money soon, so it pays to think hard before opening anything. There is no rush: an account you open at 22 works exactly like one you open at 18. Learning how orders, fees and falls work costs nothing if you practise first, and the student guide covers the choices.

What costs should you check?

Ask about account opening, annual maintenance and brokerage, and write them down. If your holdings will be small, a Basic Services Demat Account can reduce your costs: under SEBI's June 2024 circular it has no annual charge up to ₹4 lakh, and holdings can't exceed ₹10 lakh.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum age to open a demat account in India?

There is no minimum age for a demat account in a minor's name, but a guardian must operate it until 18. From 18 you can open and operate one yourself.

Do students need income proof for a demat account?

SEBI's booklet lists KYC documents for identity and address as the requirement for opening accounts. Providers may ask for more for some products, such as derivatives, so check with them.

Can a 17-year-old buy shares in India?

Not through a broker. SEBI's FAQ says a minor can't enter into a contract with a stock broker to buy or sell securities.

Kya student demat account khol sakta hai?

Haan, agar umar 18 ya usse zyada hai. Student hona rukawat nahi hai, KYC poora karna hota hai. 18 se kam ho to guardian account chalate hain aur aap khud trade nahi kar sakte.
