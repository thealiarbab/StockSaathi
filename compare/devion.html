StockSaathi vs Devion: an honest comparison

StockSaathi and Devion are both free stock market simulators for Indian teenagers, and both give you ₹1,00,000 of virtual money. They teach in different ways. Devion is course-first: lessons, quizzes and badges, with paper trading alongside. StockSaathi is practice-first: you learn by trading, and an AI coach reacts to your own decisions as you make them. This page is written by StockSaathi. Devion's details come from devion.in as it appeared on 30 September 2026 and may have changed since.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Devion if you want a structured course: modules to work through in order, quizzes that check what you learned, leaderboards to compete with classmates, and weekly progress reports for parents.

Choose StockSaathi if you learn best by doing. You'll trade from day one across both NSE and BSE, get coached on your own mistakes as they happen, relive real Indian market crashes, and be graded on the quality of your decisions instead of competing on returns. If you have never invested before, the beginner's guide covers the basics you need to start.

Both are free, so you can also try both. On StockSaathi you can browse the markets, ask the AI coach and run the COVID-19 crash replay before creating an account.
