COVID-19 Crash replay: hold or panic-sell?

India's fastest bear market. Sensex lost 35% in 33 days — and took about 8 months to fully recover. In this replay a ₹1,00,000 portfolio splits in two on day three: one investor holds, the other panic-sells. Holding finished 38.4% ahead of panic-selling.

What happened, day by day

  1. Day 0. Feb 19, 2020. Nifty at an all-time high of 12,125. The headlines mention a new virus in China — but nobody is selling. You own a diversified ₹1,00,000 portfolio across 5 large caps.
  2. Day 2. Day 3. Nifty down 2.4%. Twitter is loud. Your WhatsApp family group is louder. Fear of being stuck in a crash kicks in.
  3. Day 3. Day 3 — you panic-sold everything at ₹66,800. In that moment it feels safe. Anchored here, you will wait on the sidelines hoping to 're-enter lower'.
  4. Day 6. One week in. Nifty down 14%. The news is scary, but markets have seen scarier — and survived.
  5. Day 10. March 13, 2020. Lower circuit hit. Trading halts. If you're holding and watching a red screen, this is where most people capitulate.
  6. Day 12. Nifty down 29.6%. Most first-time investors sell here — at the most expensive emotional cost.
  7. Day 14. March 23, 2020 — the trough. ₹62,700 against your ₹1L original. From here, the market only goes one way: up. The held line is about to do something remarkable.
  8. Day 15. First sign of life. A single green day is rarely 'the bottom' — but it often is. You don't know that yet.
  9. Day 30. One month from the bottom. Held portfolio: ₹75,900. Already recovered 21% from the low. Panic-sold portfolio: still ₹66,800, compounding at 0%.
  10. Day 55. Phase shift. Stimulus announcements globally, vaccine research begins. The held line starts climbing faster.
  11. Day 61. 3 months after the crash. Held portfolio: ₹91,500 — 8.5% below start. Panic-sold: ₹66,800 — 33% below start. The held investor saw their portfolio back at the original ₹1L by September 2020. The panic-seller was still waiting 'for the right moment'.

The lesson

Crashes feel endless from the inside, and the urge to sell is strongest near the bottom. Replaying one lets you feel that pressure with virtual money and see how the decision played out.
