About StockSaathi

StockSaathi is a free stock market simulator for students and beginners in India. It was founded and built by Ali Arbab, a student developer, and launched on 19 April 2026. It gives every user ₹1,00,000 in virtual money to practise on real NSE and BSE prices.

Why StockSaathi exists

Ali Arbab started StockSaathi for two reasons. In his words: "I kept seeing people my age invest blindly, chasing tips and hype without understanding what they were buying. And the practice tools that existed weren't built for Indian beginners."

So StockSaathi is a place to make your first market mistakes for free, with virtual money, and to understand why they happened before any real money is involved.

What StockSaathi does

  • Practice trading. Buy and sell 4,000+ NSE and BSE stocks, 300+ ETFs and 8,000+ active mutual fund schemes in the Markets section, with market, limit and after-market orders.
  • Crash replays. Replay real Indian market crashes (COVID-19 in 2020, the 2008 financial crisis and demonetisation in 2016) day by day, built from real Nifty 50 closes, and compare holding with panic-selling.
  • An AI coach. Saathi checks every trade for nine common investing mistakes, such as panic-selling and FOMO buying, and explains what it noticed in your own numbers, in English or Hinglish.
  • Guides. Plain-English guides to how the Indian stock market works, written for complete beginners.
  • A report card. Your report card grades the quality of your decisions, not just your returns.
  • No leaderboard. Nobody is ranked by returns, by design: the point is better decisions, not short-term wins.

How StockSaathi is built

Stock prices come from Yahoo Finance, with NSE's official end-of-day bhavcopy as a second source, and mutual fund NAVs come from AMFI. Prices can be delayed, so StockSaathi is not a live trading feed. Limit orders and after-market orders are matched on the server, so they fill even when the app is closed. The AI coach explains concepts and reflects on your own decisions; it never tells you what to buy or sell.

How does StockSaathi check what it explains?

StockSaathi's guides link the primary sources they rely on, such as SEBI, NSE, BSE or AMFI, and market figures are worked out from daily closing prices the guide names. Replays and stock pages are built from the same data the app uses, and the build refuses a replay whose numbers don't match that data, or a guide whose key figures don't.

The AI coach can make mistakes, so check anything important against the official sources before relying on it. If you find an error anywhere on StockSaathi, write to grievance@stocksaathi.co.in and it will be corrected.
