A free stock market game for school and college students in India

StockSaathi is a free virtual stock market game for Indian teenagers aged 13 to 18, whether you're in class 8 or in your first year of college. You get ₹1,00,000 of virtual money, real NSE and BSE stocks at real market prices, and an AI coach that points out beginner mistakes while you make them. It is free, there is no real money anywhere in it, and you can try most of it before you sign up.

For students

You probably hear about stocks, SIPs and "the market" long before anyone explains them. StockSaathi lets you find out how it actually works by doing it. Buy a few shares of companies you know, place a limit order, hold through a bad week and see what happens. If you panic-sell after a drop, the coach will ask you to look at the history first. If you put most of your money into one stock, it will tell you. Your report card grades your decisions, not your luck, so you can get better at investing without needing anyone to beat. New to all of this? Start with the beginner's guide.

For parents

  • Virtual money only. Every account starts with ₹1,00,000 of practice money that can't be deposited, withdrawn or converted into anything real.
  • No payments. StockSaathi is free and never asks for card, UPI or bank details.
  • No ads, no data selling. Personal data is not sold or used for advertising. See the privacy policy.
  • No tips. The AI coach explains concepts and reflects on the student's own decisions. It never recommends buying or selling a security.
  • Delete any time. To have an account and all its data deleted, write to grievance@stocksaathi.co.in.

For teachers

Every student gets a free individual account, and no teacher account is needed. When students sign up they can add their school and a class or batch code. The crash replays work well for discussion: run the COVID-19 or 2008 replay together and ask the class when they would have sold. Students can also send each other virtual cash by @username or redeem code, which is handy for small classroom challenges. There is no leaderboard ranking students by returns, on purpose: the lesson is decision-making, not gambling on short-term wins.

What students can practise

  • Buying and selling 4,000+ NSE and BSE stocks, plus ETFs and mutual funds.
  • Market, limit and after-market orders.
  • Reading candlestick charts from one day to the full price history.
  • Spotting nine common investing mistakes, with the AI coach's help.
  • Living through real Indian market crashes in Time Travel.
  • Asking Saathi, the AI coach, about any finance concept, in English or Hinglish.
