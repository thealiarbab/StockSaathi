Contact StockSaathi

StockSaathi has three email addresses, each read by a person. Pick the one that matches what you need and you'll reach the right inbox first time.

Which address should I write to?

  • Support and data requests: grievance@stocksaathi.co.in. Problems with your account, a trade or an order; requests to see, correct or delete your data; complaints and takedown notices. This is also the address of StockSaathi's Grievance Officer.
  • Business, partnerships and press: business@stocksaathi.co.in. Partnerships, media and press questions, and schools that want to work with StockSaathi.
  • Students and schools: ambassador@stocksaathi.co.in. Bringing StockSaathi to your school or running a class competition. This inbox belongs to Devansh Singh Chauhan, StockSaathi's brand ambassador.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Write to grievance@stocksaathi.co.in from the email address on your account and ask for your account and its data to be deleted. The privacy policy explains what StockSaathi stores and why.

Does StockSaathi give investment advice?

No. StockSaathi is an educational simulator that uses only virtual money. It is not a SEBI-registered broker, investment adviser or research analyst, and is not affiliated with SEBI, NSE or BSE, so it can't answer questions about which stocks to buy or sell, with real money or otherwise.
