Hafta 1: share market ke zaroori shabd kya hain?

Pehle hafte sirf samjho, kharido mat. Share matlab kisi company ka chhota sa hissa. NSE aur BSE India ke do bade stock exchange hain. Nifty 50 NSE ki 50 badi companies ka index hai; log "market upar gaya" bolte hain to aksar isi ki baat karte hain. Demat account wo account hai jisme shares electronically rakhe jaate hain.

Apni pasand ki 5 companies chuno jinke products tum use karte ho, aur hafte bhar unki price dekho. Note karo ki price roz kyun badalti hai. Zyada detail ke liye beginner's guide padho, ya Saathi coach se Hinglish mein poochho.

Hafta 2: pehla practice trade kaise karein?

Ab chhote trades lagao. StockSaathi par account banao; ₹1,00,000 ka virtual balance milega. Ek company ke kuch shares market order se khareedo, jo turant us price par chalta hai jo market mein mil rahi hai.

Phir doosri company par limit order lagao, jo sirf tumhari di hui price ya usse behtar price par chalta hai. NSE ki investor material bhi in dono ko aise hi samjhaati hai.

Ek baat yaad rakho: saara paisa ek hi company mein mat daalo. StockSaathi ka coach batata hai jab ek stock tumhare portfolio ka 40% se zyada ho jaata hai. Order types ki poori jaankari market, limit aur AMO guide mein hai.

Hafta 3: crash mein kya hota hai?

Teesre hafte ek asli crash ko replay karo. COVID-19 crash replay mein ₹1,00,000 ka portfolio 2020 ke asli Nifty 50 closes par chalta hai, aur tum dekhte ho ki teesre din bechne waale aur hold karne waale ke saath kya hua. Note karo ki kis din tumhe bechne ka sabse zyada mann hua, aur us din tumhare dimaag mein kya chal raha tha. Crash ko padhna aur crash ko jheelna do alag baatein hain, chahe paisa virtual hi kyun na ho.

Replay khatam hone ke baad teen cheezein likho: sabse bura din kaunsa tha, tumne kya kiya, aur agar wahi fall dobara aaye to kya alag karoge. Isse tumhe pata chalta hai ki tum girte market mein kaise react karte ho, aur ye jaankari asli paisa lagane se pehle milna sabse sasta tareeka hai.

November 2010 ke high se Nifty 50 ki har poori ho chuki 10% girawat wapas recover hui, median 200 trading days mein, lekin ye guarantee nahi hai. SEBI ki investor booklet kehti hai ki market sentiment par nahi, company aur uske financials ko dekh kar faisla karo. Crash replay wahi practice karwata hai.

Hafta 4: apni galtiyan kaise pakdein?

Aakhri hafte apne trades ko dobara dekho. StockSaathi ka coach har trade ko 9 aam galtiyon ke liye check karta hai, jaise panic-selling (girte price mein dar ke maare bechna) aur FOMO buying (bahut upar ja chuke stock ke peeche bhaagna). Jab koi trade aisa lagta hai, to order ke baad ek chhoti note aati hai tumhare apne numbers ke saath. Report card tumhare faislon ko grade karta hai, sirf profit ko nahi.

Har galti ke baare mein ek line likho: maine kya kiya, kyun kiya, aur agli baar kya alag karunga. Ye notebook tumhara sabse kaam ka investing teacher hai, kyunki ye tumhare apne faislon se bani hai.

18 saal se pehle asli investing ho sakti hai?

Seedhe nahi. SEBI ke hisaab se minor ke naam par demat account khul sakta hai jise guardian chalate hain, lekin minor khud broker ke saath shares khareedne ya bechne ka contract nahi kar sakta. Isliye practice ka ye time sabse sahi hai. Poori jaankari teenager demat account guide mein hai.

Galat salah aur tips groups se kaise bachein?

Sabse seedha niyam: jo koi pakka profit ka wada kare, usse door raho. SEBI apni investor guidance mein saaf kehta hai ki hot tips par faisla mat karo, aur ye bhi ki hot tips phailana illegal hai jise SEBI ko report kiya ja sakta hai. Herd yaani bheed ke peeche chalne se bhi SEBI mana karta hai.

Telegram ya WhatsApp group ka screenshot koi saboot nahi hota. Jab bhi koi tip mile, khud se poochho: ye company kya karti hai, aur main ye share kyun khareed raha hoon? StockSaathi ka coach kabhi tips nahi deta, wo sirf samjhata hai.

4 hafte ke baad aage kya seekhein?

Ab ek topic gehrai se lo. Nifty aur Sensex samjho, phir panic selling wali guide padho, aur apni practice portfolio ko dobara dekho. Asli paisa lagane ki koi jaldi nahi hai: seekhne ka maqsad ye hai ki jab bhi tum shuru karo, samajh kar karo.
